A tiny agreement

Before you use HyperStream, here's the simple version.

Camera: HyperStream needs camera access to create the live visual hologram. Your browser controls camera permission.
Your content: Recordings and captures stay in your browser unless you choose to save or share them.
Sharing: When you tap Share, you choose what app or person receives the file. Check the destination before sending anything private.
People: Only scan or share people when you have the appropriate permission. Avoid capturing private or sensitive information.
