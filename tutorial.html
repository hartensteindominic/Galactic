HyperStream ✨

Turn the world around you into a little holographic world.

1

Pick your camera

Face Camera starts automatically. Use Back Camera for objects, rooms, and wider scenes.

2

Tap Start Scanning

Move slowly around your subject. Smooth movement gives the hologram more useful visual information.

3

Shape the hologram

Use Detail for point density, Depth and Relief for 3D shape, Brightness and Contrast for the look, then tune Smoothing and Contour.

4

Record it

Once scanning is active, Record lights up. Record a short pass, then use Replay to see the captured sequence.

5

Share ✨

Tap Share to use your iPhone share sheet. Instagram can be selected when it supports the shared file.

Open HyperStream
Tip: bright, evenly lit subjects usually produce cleaner point clouds. HyperStream is a visual capture effect, not a measurement-grade 3D scanner.
